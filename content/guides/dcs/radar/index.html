--- title: Interactive Radar Trainer (F/A-18C) summary: An interactive tool for learning the basics of the air-to-air radar in the DCS F/A-18C Hornet. tags: - digital combat simulator weight: 6 date: 2026-06-01 ---

This tool shows a single snapshot of an air-to-air radar picture in the F/A-18C. You fly straight and level at 20,000 ft; a few contacts are scattered in the airspace ahead. Use the HOTAS controls to point the radar and step through its settings, and watch how the same contacts appear across the AZ/EL, SA, and ATK RDR formats and in the 3D view. Reload the page for a new picture.
